**Ant Colonies and Social Structure**

• Ants have the most advanced society in the insect world, with colonies that build complex ant-cities and follow strict social laws.
• In some societies, an ant's life can be one of three things:
	+ Worker
	+ Soldier
	+ Queen or reproductive individual

**Estimates of Ant Population**

• Estimates suggest there are approximately one million ants per person on earth.

**Ant Social Dynamics**

• Some species of ants practice slavery by enslaving members of rival ant species to provide food and labor.
