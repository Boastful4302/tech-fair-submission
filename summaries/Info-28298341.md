**Cardiocondyla Evolution**

• Cardiocondyla is a myrmicine genus with diverse colony structures, reproductive behaviors, and lifespans.
• Ancestral life history of Cardiocondyla included:
	+ Multiple short-lived queens
	+ Polyphenic males (winged and wingless) with lethal combat for females
• Derived traits include:
	+ Single queening
	+ Queen polyphenism
	+ Loss of winged males
	+ Tolerance among wingless males
• Changes in nesting habits, colony size, and environmental shifts contributed to trait evolution.
