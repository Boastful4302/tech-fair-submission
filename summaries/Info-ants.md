**Global Distribution and Abundance**

• Ants make up at least 15% of terrestrial animal biomass globally.
• In tropical areas like the Amazon, ants comprise 25% or more of terrestrial animal biomass.

**Regional Variations in Population Density**

• A single hectare (2.5 acres) in the Amazon rainforest can contain 8 million or more ants.
• In Côte d'Ivoire's savanna, a hectare contains an estimated 20 million ants.

**Global Distribution by Continent**

• Native ant species exist on almost all continents except:
  • Antarctica
  • Greenland
  • Iceland
  • Parts of Polynesia
