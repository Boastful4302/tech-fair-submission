**Discovery and Classification**

* A new ant species, **Martialis heureka (gen. et sp. nov.)**, has been discovered in the Amazon rainforest.
* It represents the sister lineage to all extant ants (**Hymenoptera: Formicidae**) and is part of a new subfamily, **Martialinae (subfam. nov.)**.

**Phylogenetic Analysis**

* The phylogenetic position was inferred from 5 nuclear genes sequenced from a single leg.
* Previous studies were affected by long-branch attraction artifacts, but this study reduces the likelihood of such errors.

**Lineage Characteristics and Survival**

* Early-diverging ant lineages are thought to be cryptic, hypogaeic foragers that have survived in stable environments over great spans of time.
