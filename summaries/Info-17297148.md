**Introduction**

* Two ant species, **Linepithema humile** and **Aphaenogaster cockerelli**, were studied to examine the role of structural complexity in hydrocarbon recognition cues.

**Hydrocarbon Composition**

* Cuticular hydrocarbons are composed of molecules from three structural classes:
	+ n-alkanes
	+ methyl-branched alkanes
	+ n-alkenes

**Aggressive Species Recognition Response**

* Both species required mixtures of at least two hydrocarbon classes to elicit an aggressive species recognition response.
* No single class of hydrocarbons was more important than others in eliciting a response.

**Species-Specific Recognition**

* **Linepithema humile** recognized conspecifics when nestmate hydrocarbon profiles were supplemented with n-alkanes.
