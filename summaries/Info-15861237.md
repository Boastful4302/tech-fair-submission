**Nest Architecture**

* Composed of descending shafts (4-6 cm diameter) and horizontal chambers
* Shafts form helices with varying angles (15-70 degrees) depending on depth
* Superficial chambers (< 15 cm deep) are distinct from deeper chambers
* Chamber area is greatest in the upper reaches of the nest, decreasing with depth

**Chamber Distribution and Growth**

* Vertical spacing between chambers increases with depth
* Total chamber area is top-heavy, with half occurring in the top quarter of the nest
* Nests grow by simultaneous deepening, addition of new chambers/shafts, and enlargement of existing chambers
* Vertical spacing between chambers remains similar at all nest sizes

**Worker Behavior and Carbon Dioxide Gradient**

* Older workers engage in more digging and assume positions in the upper parts of the nest
* Younger workers prefer high carbon dioxide concentrations, which may explain their preference for digging lower areas of the nest
* CO2 concentrations increase 5-fold between surface and nest depths
* May serve as a template for depth information, influencing worker behavior and nest architecture

**Demographic and Structural Features**

* Vertical age-distribution (a demographic characteristic)
* Top-heaviness of the nest's architecture (a structural feature)
