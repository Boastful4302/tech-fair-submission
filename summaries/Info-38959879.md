**Antimicrobial Strategies in Ants**

* Some ant species have lost their metapleural gland, which produces antimicrobial secretions, relying on other defense mechanisms instead.

**Behavioral Treatment of Injuries**

* Camponotus floridanus ants use behavioral treatment for wound care:
	+ Amputation (with 75% improved survival rate) for femur injuries
	+ Increased wound care for distal tibia injuries
* Immediate amputation after pathogen exposure improves survival in infected tibia injuries (80% vs 40% without)

**Anatomical Considerations**

* Muscles responsible for leg hemolymph circulation are predominantly located in the femur, suggesting that femur injuries provide time for amputations before pathogen spread.

**Comparative Analysis of Injury Response**

* Ants do not amputate legs with distal tibia injuries and focus on wound care
* Amputation is more effective than wound care alone for improving survival rates
