**Introduction**

* Fossils of social insects from the Mesozoic era are extremely rare, but a significant discovery has been made in New Jersey amber.

**Species Description**

* The fossils belong to the species Sphecomyrma freyi.
* Dated to approximately 100 million years ago during the Upper Cretaceous age.
* Represent the first undisputed remains of social insects from the Mesozoic era.

**Classification and Evolutionary Significance**

* Assigned with certainty to aculeate Hymenoptera, a group that includes ants and wasps.
* Forms link between nonsocial tiphiid wasps and most primitive myrmecioid ants.
* Represents a new subfamily (Sphecomyrminae), more primitive than any previously known ant group.

**Implications**

* This discovery fills a significant gap in our understanding of the evolution of social insects.
