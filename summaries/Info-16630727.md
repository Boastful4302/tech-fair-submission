**Classification of Ants**

• The traditional classification of the ant subfamily Ponerinae has been revised, grouping it into six poneromorph subfamilies.
• Molecular tree analysis using 28S rRNA gene sequences confirms the monophyly of Formicidae with strong support (100% PB and PP=1.00).

**Phylogenetic Relationships**

• The Leptanillinae are sister to all other ants, with moderate support (PB=62, PP=93).
• Key relationships include:
	+ Ectatomminae and Heteroponerinae are closely related to Formicoid subfamilies (PB=96, PP=100).
	+ Amblyoponinae, Paraponerinae, Ponerinae, and Proceratiinae appear as distinct lineages at the base of the tree.
	+ Platythyrea is a sister group to Ponerini within Ponerinae.
	+ Heteroponerinae are associated with Ectatomminae (PB=98, PP=100).
	+ Probolomyrmex belongs to Proceratiinae (PB=98, PP=100).

**Poneromorph Subfamilies**

• While poneromorph subfamilies show polyphyly, some lineages are more closely related than others.
