**Species and Sample Size**

• Nine Bruneian Camponotus species in the cylindricus complex were analyzed.

**Chemical Composition**

• Species produce mixtures of polyacetate-derived aromatics, including:
	+ Hydroxyacetophenones with pH-dependent color changes
	+ Aliphatic hydrocarbons and alcohols

**Unique Compounds**

• Three species contain previously unreported compounds:
	+ (6R)-2,6-dimethyl-(2E)-octen-1,8-dioic acid (9)
	+ (3S)-8-hydroxycitronellic acid (10a)

**Mandibular Glands**

• Workers have hypertrophied mandibular glands that rupture to release contents suicidally from the head.
