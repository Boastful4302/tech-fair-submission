**Taxonomy and Allergy Information**

• The article focuses on reinforcing knowledge about fire ants (Solenopsis invicta and richteri), including their taxonomy, origin, and distribution.
• Fire ants are responsible for causing anaphylaxis reactions in some individuals.

**Key Allergens and Immunotherapy**

• Four key allergens in Sol i venom have been isolated: 
  • Sol i 1
  • Sol i 2
  • Sol i 3
  • Sol i 4

**Diagnosis and Treatment**

• Fire ant allergy-specific immunotherapy can reduce the risk of systemic reactions.
• Clinical studies compare the safety and efficacy of:
  • Fire ant venom for diagnosis and treatment
  • Whole body extract for diagnosis and treatment
