**Navigation Abilities of Desert Ants**

• Researchers studied desert ants (Cataglyphis fortis) to understand their navigation abilities.
• Local variations in ground properties can serve as landmarks for ants, aiding in navigation.
• In experiments, ants were trained on a channel floor with altered optical and tactile properties near the nest entrance:
	+ Tactile changes: physical modifications to the ground surface
	+ Optical changes: visual cues affecting light reflection or absorption
• When tested in a parallel channel, ants recognized the landmark and centered their search around it.
• Ants require both correct visual and tactile information to recognize ground structures as familiar landmarks.

**Landmark Learning**

• Physical ground properties can be learned and used to define landmarks, such as the nest position.
• This learning process relies on ants' ability to integrate multiple sensory inputs (visual and tactile) to create a mental map of their environment.
