**Fire Ant Venom Dispersal**

* The imported fire ant, Solenopsis invicta Buren (Myrmicinae), has developed a unique behavior called "gaster flagging" to disperse venom through the air.
* This behavior involves raising and vibrating its gaster, releasing up to 500 ng of venom into the air.
* There are two main contexts for venom release:
	+ Foraging workers use airborne venom to repel heterospecifics encountered in the foraging arena, possibly as a defense mechanism.
	+ Brood tenders dispense smaller quantities (approximately 1 ng) of venom to the brood surface, likely serving an antibacterial function.
* Workers exhibit control over the quantity of venom released, and this behavior is context-dependent rather than fixed to a specific caste or time.
