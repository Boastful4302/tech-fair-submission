**Mid-Cretaceous Amber Fossil Discovery**

• Researchers studied a Mid-Cretaceous amber fossil containing a pupa and adult of a stem ant species.
• The presence of the pupa suggests that wingless females may have been "workers" that cooperated with others.

**Species Description and Classification**

• A new species, Gerontoformica sternorhabda sp. nov., was described using high-resolution micro-CT scans.
• The classification of the genus Gerontoformica was revised and clarified.

**Evolutionary Significance**

• Winged-wingless diphenism, where ants had varying dispersal capacities, may be an ancestral trait to all ants.
• The study provides evidence for the evolution of eusociality in stem ants.
• The findings were published in the Zoological Journal of the Linnean Society (August 2022).
