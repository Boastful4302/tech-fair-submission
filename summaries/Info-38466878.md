**Ant Diversity and Evolution**

• The ant species is incredibly diverse with ~14,000 extant species, having undergone significant evolutionary changes throughout its history.

**Methodology and Analysis**

• A comprehensive analysis of the ant fossil record (~24,000 individual occurrences) and neontological data (~14,000 occurrences) was conducted to understand drivers of their diversity dynamics.
• Four hypotheses were tested: co-diversification, competitive extinction, hyper-specialization, and buffered extinction.

**Key Findings and Drivers**

• Three distinct diversification periods were identified:
	+ Latest Cretaceous
	+ Eocene
	+ Oligo-Miocene
• One extinction period was observed: Late Cretaceous
• Key drivers of ant diversity dynamics include:
	+ Co-diversification
	+ Buffered extinction
	+ Hyper-specialization
• Environmental changes, particularly the Angiosperm Terrestrial Revolution (ATR), played a critical role in buffering ants against extinction and favoring their diversification.

**Evolutionary Pressures**

• Stem ants declined due to their hyper-specialized morphology, which limited their ability to adapt to changing environments.
