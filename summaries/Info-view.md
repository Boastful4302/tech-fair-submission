**BugGuide Gathering Events**

* The BugGuide Gathering is an annual event that brings together entomologists and insect enthusiasts to share knowledge and experiences in the field of entomology.
* The first mentioned gathering took place at Smoky Mountains University of Tennessee Biological Field Station on August 8-10, 2008.
* This event was preceded by a previous gathering held in Minnesota in 2007.
