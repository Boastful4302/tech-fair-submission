**Social Reproduction in Cataglyphis hispanica**

* The desert ant **Cataglyphis hispanica** has evolved a unique form of social reproduction called social hybridogenesis.
* This phenomenon involves interbreeding between two distinct genetic lineages, resulting in the production of workers through sexual reproduction.
* Male and female sexuals are produced through parthenogenesis, a type of asexual reproduction that does not involve male gametes.
* Workers are "pure hybridogens", meaning they inherit both maternal and paternal genomes, while only their mothers' genes are passed on to new queens through the germline.
* Queens use both sexual and asexual reproductive strategies:
	+ Sexual reproduction is used for colony growth, producing offspring with mixed genetic makeup.
	+ Parthenogenesis is used to produce new queens, ensuring that they inherit only maternal genes.
