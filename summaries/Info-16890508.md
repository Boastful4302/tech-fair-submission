**Study on Ant Communication**

• A study on communication in ants was published by Duncan E. Jackson and Francis L.W. Ratnieks in 2006.
• The study, titled "Curr Biol" (doi: 10.1016/j.cub.2006.07.015), investigated the role of pheromones in ant behavior.
• Ants use pheromones to convey information about food sources and other types of information, such as danger or food location.
• Pheromone trails are crucial for ants' ability to locate and follow paths to food sources.
• The research demonstrated that ants use different types of pheromones to signal distinct types of information.
