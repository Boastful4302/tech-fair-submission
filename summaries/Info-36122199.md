**Ant Abundance and Distribution**

• The global abundance of ants is estimated at almost 20 x 10^15 individuals.
• Ground-dwelling ants account for over 3 x 10^15 individuals, while arboreal ants make up the remaining population.

**Regional Concentrations**

• Tropical and subtropical regions have the highest concentrations of ground-dwelling ants.
• The density of leaf-litter ants is highest in forests, while arid regions have the highest numbers of actively ground-foraging ants.

**Comparison to Other Biomasses**

• Ants constitute a significant portion of Earth's biomass, equivalent to about 20% of human biomass and exceeding the combined biomass of wild birds and mammals.
• The biomass of ants is approximately 12 megatons of dry carbon.
