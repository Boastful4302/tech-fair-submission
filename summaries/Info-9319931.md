**Trap-Jaw Mechanism**

• The Dacetini tribe of ants has evolved "trap-jaws", characterized by extremely fast snapping mandibles, allowing for rapid prey capture.

**Strike Mechanism**

• Two genera in the Dacetini tribe employ this mechanism: large Daceton and small Strumigenys.
• The strike takes less than 2.5 ms to execute.
• A catapult-like mechanism generates rapid movement, involving a trigger system that activates within 5 ms.
• This mechanism involves a labrum hair contact that triggers the fast trigger muscles.

**Neurological Control**

• Giant sensory and motor neurons in the labral neuromere control the trap-jaw reflex.
• These neurons contribute to the short latency of the strike, ranging from 2.5-5 ms.

**Evolutionary Comparison**

• Convergent evolution is observed between Dacetini trap-jaws and those of Odontomachus ants.
