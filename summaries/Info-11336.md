**Ant Evolution and Diversity**

• Ants have a history spanning over 100 million years with approximately 14,000 described living species.
• They play a crucial role in terrestrial systems, particularly in the tropics, serving as models for evolution, ecology, and ecosystem monitoring studies.

**Taxonomic Relationships and Origins**

• The Aculeata ants are thought to be the sister group of Apoidea, with their ancestor (the Formicidae) inhabiting the northern hemisphere during the early Cretaceous period.
• This ancestral species was likely present in both hemispheres but became extinct in the southern hemisphere.

**Neotropical Region Diversity**

• The Neotropical region is home to 137 genera and around 3,100 species of ants.
• This region may have been a "cradle" and "museum" for ant groups, explaining their high diversity and endemism levels.

**Study Details**

• A study on Neotropical ants was conducted by Fernando Fernandez, Roberto Ariel Guerrero, and Andrés Fernando Sánchez Restrepo.
• The study was published in Revista Colombiana de Entomología, Volume 47, Issue 1 (2021), spanning page numbers 1-20.
