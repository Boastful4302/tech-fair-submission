**Phylogeny and Evolutionary History of Ants**

• The study of ant anatomy, behavior, and DNA sequences over the past two decades has greatly clarified their phylogeny.
• Fossil records from the Cretaceous and Paleogene periods have helped date major evolutionary radiations in ant lineages.

**Key Ecological Adaptations and Radiations**

• Initial radiation at the subfamily level occurred in forest ground litter and soil during the mid-Cretaceous period, coinciding with the rise of angiosperms (flowering plants).
• By the Paleogene period, these early ant lineages had advanced to dominance in tropical forests.
• Further expansion into canopy and xeric environments was facilitated by dietary changes.
