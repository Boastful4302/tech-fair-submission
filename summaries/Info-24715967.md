**Thermoregulation in Ant Colonies**

• Temperature has a significant impact on ant biology, influencing metabolic rate, growth, and development.
• High inner nest temperatures can accelerate sexual brood development, thereby boosting colony fitness.
• Insect societies outperform solitary insects in thermoregulation due to complex nesting behaviors.
**Thermoregulatory Strategies:**
• Ants employ two primary strategies:
	+ Relocating brood according to thermal gradients within the mound.
	+ Maintaining high internal temperatures through unique nest material properties (e.g., Formica wood ant genus).
**Additional Heat Sources:**
• Metabolic activity from ants and associated microorganisms contribute to heat production.
