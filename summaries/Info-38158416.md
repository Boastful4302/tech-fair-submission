**Wound Treatment by Megaponera analis Ants**

• Megaponera analis (M. analis) ants are capable of diagnosing and treating infected wounds, resulting in a 90% reduction in mortality.
• The ants use antimicrobial compounds and proteins from the metapleural gland to treat infections.
• Chemical analyses have linked wound infection to specific changes in cuticular hydrocarbon profiles.
• The metapleural gland is responsible for producing these antimicrobial compounds used in wound treatment.
