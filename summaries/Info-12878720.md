**The Role of Genetics in Social Insects**

• The concept of division of labor is essential for society's success, as seen in the organization of social insects like ants.
• Physically polymorphic worker castes with morphological adaptations to specific roles are present in social insects, such as Acromyrmex echinatior leaf-cutting ants.

**Genetic Influence on Worker Caste Development**

• Previous research suggested that nongenetic control (environmental cues) over worker caste development in social insects.
• However, a new study on Acromyrmex echinatior leaf-cutting ant reveals genetic influence on worker caste development.
• Individuals from different patrilines within the same colony have varying propensities to become minor or major workers.

**Interplay between Genetics and Environment**

• Worker caste destiny results from interaction between genetic and environmental factors, challenging previous assumptions about nongenetic control.
