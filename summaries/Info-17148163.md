**Ants' Nest Site Evaluation**

• Ants exhibit hygienic behavior by rejecting potential nest sites containing dead ants, indicating concern for colony health and survival.
• Dead ants pose risks to the colony's well-being, suggesting disease as a significant selective force in shaping ant behavior.
• This discovery highlights the importance of considering hygiene in evaluating nest site quality.

**Factors Influencing Nest Site Selection**

• Disease prevalence is a key factor in ants' evaluation of potential nest sites.
• Ants prioritize avoiding dead individuals and potential disease transmission to ensure colony survival.
• The presence of dead ants may outweigh other benefits, such as resource availability or proximity to food sources.
