**Ancient Ant Fossils**

* The oldest known ant fossils date back to the Mesozoic era, approximately 100 million years ago.
* Despite their ancient origin, ants were likely a minor component of insect life at that time due to limited fossil evidence.

**Types of Ancient Ant Fossils**

* Two primary types exist:
	+ As imprints in rock, which have inherent biases and limitations.
	+ As inclusions in fossilized resins (amber), also with inherent biases.
