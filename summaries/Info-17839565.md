**Chromosome Number and Homology**

• A new sibling species of Myrmecia pilosula has been discovered with a chromosome number of n = 1.
• Males have only one chromosome, as is typical in Hymenoptera, and their somatic cells are haploid.

**Cytological Analysis**

• C-banding techniques confirm that the two chromosomes of workers are homologous (i.e., identical or nearly identical).
• The new species has potential for significant contributions to both laboratory and field studies on gene organization.
