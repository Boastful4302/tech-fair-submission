**Evolutionary History of Army Ants**

• A phylogenetic study by researchers at the Allan Wilson Centre for Molecular Ecology and Evolution (Massey University) has shed new light on the evolutionary history of army ants.
• The study has challenged previous understanding of the origin of circular mill formation, a behavior where ants create a self-sustaining ring-like structure that can be fatal to individuals trapped inside.

**Evolutionary Pattern in Army Ant Species**

• Circular mill formation is not an isolated phenomenon, but rather part of a larger evolutionary pattern observed in army ant species.
• This suggests that the behavior may have evolved as an adaptation to specific ecological pressures or social structures within these species.
