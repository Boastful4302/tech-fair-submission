**Etymology of Formic**

• The term "formic" originated from the Latin word "formica," meaning "ant."
• It was coined in 1791 to refer to formic acid obtained by distilling red ants.

**History of Formic Acid Discovery**

• Andreas Sigismund Marggraf first prepared pure formic acid in 1749 using a process of distillation.
• The compound is also found in other natural sources, including:
	+ Nettles
	+ Bee stings
