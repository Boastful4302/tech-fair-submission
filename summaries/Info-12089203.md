**Navigation Strategies in Desert Ants**

• Desert ants (Cataglyphis) employ path integration and landmarks to navigate during foraging excursions.
• Research involved training ants to find their nest among four alternative positions within a four-cylinder landmark array.
• The landmark array consisted of identical cylinders arranged in a square, with the nest located at the southeast corner.
• When released from the trained compass direction (southeast), ants searched most intensely at the fictive nest position.
• Ants released from alternative directions of approach combined visual snapshot memory and celestial reference information to search at two specific positions.
