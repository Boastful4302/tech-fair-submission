**Ant Navigation Systems**

• Cataglyphis fortis (desert ants) employ multiple navigation systems: path integration, a celestial compass, and an odometer to monitor their position relative to the nest.
• As foraging distance increases, ants tend to underestimate their traveled distances by more than 10%.
• The relationship between training distance and estimated distance can be described using both logarithmic and exponential models.
• The ant's odometric undershooting may be adaptive, helping them focus on familiar landmarks during homing behavior.
