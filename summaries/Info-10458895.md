**Task Specialization in Desert Leaf-Cutter Ants**

* Desert leaf-cutter ants exhibit individual task specialization in undertaking behavior.
* Workers show varying levels of engagement in corpse removal:
	+ Some workers remove dead ants more frequently than expected based on encounter rates.
	+ Opportunity to perform tasks does not explain individual worker specialization.
* The underlying cause of undertaking specialization is not related to spatial structuring of workers or their opportunity to perform tasks.
* Instead, internal physiological or genetic factors govern undertaking specialization in these ants.
* Undertaking specialists are more successful and efficient at removing corpses, especially in larger colonies.
