**Tandem Running Behavior in Temnothorax albipennis**

• The ant species Temnothorax albipennis exhibits tandem running behavior to guide other ants from the nest to food sources.
• Tandem running involves a complex exchange of signals between two ants, controlling both speed and course corrections.
• This phenomenon demonstrates teaching in non-human animals, with bidirectional feedback occurring between the leader (teacher) and follower (pupil).
• Research suggests that teaching may be influenced by the value of information conveyed, rather than being constrained by brain size.

**Key Findings**

• The ability to teach is not exclusive to large-brained species.
• Informational value plays a significant role in shaping teaching behavior.
• Tandem running provides valuable insights into the cognitive and social abilities of Temnothorax albipennis.
