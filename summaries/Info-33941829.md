**Mate Selection in Ants**

• Sexual reproduction in organisms involves the crucial choice of mating partner driven by sexual selection.
• In some ant species, such as Cardiocondyla elegans, workers play a key role in promoting outbreeding through assisted mate transfer.
• Ant workers (Cardiocondyla elegans) engage in assisted outbreeding by carrying female sexuals 1-5 meters and dropping them into other nests to mate with wingless males.
• The research indicates that ants may selectively choose recipient colonies for assisted outbreeding, enabling indirect female choice.
• This phenomenon is analogous to human matchmaking, exemplifying the concept of third-party sexual selection in natural history.
