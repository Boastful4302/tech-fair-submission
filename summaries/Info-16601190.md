**Phylogenetic Analysis of Ants**

• A large-scale molecular phylogeny was created using sequence data from 6 gene regions (4.5 kilobases) covering 139 ant genera.
• The study included 19 out of the 20 subfamilies of ants (Hymenoptera: Formicidae).
• Phylogenetic analysis revealed that all but two subfamilies were monophyletic, indicating a common evolutionary origin.
**Divergence and Fossil Records**

• Fossil records indicate that most ant subfamilies diverged in the Late Cretaceous to Early Eocene period (~66-56 million years ago).
• Contrary to previous estimates, divergence times suggest that some ant subfamilies arose as far back as ~125-100 million years ago.
