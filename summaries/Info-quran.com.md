**Translation and Context**

* The translation is from Surah An-Naml (The Ant) in the Quran, specifically verse 27:1.
* The Arabic text reads طس تلك ايات القران وكتاب مبين ١, which translates to "Tales of the past Prophets, and stories of the children of Israel."

**Description of the Quran**

* The Quran is described as the clear Book (Ṭâ-Sĩn), emphasizing its clarity and accuracy.
* This description highlights the Quran's emphasis on its own authenticity and reliability.
