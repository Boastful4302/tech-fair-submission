**Global Distribution of the Argentine Ant**

• The Argentine ant (Linepithema humile) has formed extensive, interconnected supercolonies across continents through human activity.
• Each continent is home to a single large supercolony, occasionally accompanied by smaller secondary colonies.

**Intercontinental Behavioral Similarities and Differences**

• Intercontinental behavioral analyses demonstrate that supercolonies recognize and accept each other as part of a global entity.
• Genetic profiles are similar across continents, suggesting minimal genetic diversity among dominant populations.

**Genetic and Chemical Similarities**

• The chemical profiles of Argentine ant colonies also show similarity across continents.

**Exceptions to Global Behavioral Acceptance**

• Aggression is observed towards ants from South Africa and some smaller secondary colonies in Hawaii and California.
• Despite extensive distribution, the dominant Argentine ant populations have evolved little over 100 years.
