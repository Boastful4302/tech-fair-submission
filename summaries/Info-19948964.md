**Ant Bacterial Communities**

* A study surveyed 283 ant species from 18 subfamilies using molecular techniques to investigate their bacterial communities.
* Herbivorous "turtle ants" (Cephalotes and Procryptocerus) commonly harbor bacteria from clades including Burkholderiales, Pseudomonadales, Rhizobiales, Verrucomicrobiales, and Xanthomonadales.

**Rhizobiales Gut Symbionts**

* Lab-reared Cephalotes varians revealed that microbes from the Rhizobiales clade are symbiotic residents of ant guts.
* Statistical analysis shows a strong relationship between herbivory and the prevalence of Rhizobiales gut symbionts within ant genera.

**Evolutionary Origins**

* At least 5 independent origins of symbioses between herbivorous ants and Rhizobiales were identified in the ant phylogeny.
* The results support the hypothesis that bacteria have facilitated convergent evolution of herbivory across the ants.
