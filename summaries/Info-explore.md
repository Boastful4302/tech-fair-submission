**Ant Body Parts**

* Mandibles: used for eating, cleaning, and fighting
* Mouth: also used for cleaning and nest maintenance
* Eyes: vary in size and shape depending on ant species
* Head: different shapes and sizes indicate diet and lifestyle
* Mesosoma: contains muscles powering three pairs of legs for running
* Legs: end in hooked claws for climbing and hanging
* Gaster: houses heart, digestive system, and chemical weaponry (sting or acid spray)
* Petiole and post-petiole: flexible junction allowing ants to bend gaster forward

**Worker Ants**

* Perform various jobs to keep an ant colony alive and healthy
* Different parts of the body serve specific functions:
	+ Minor workers: smaller, perform general labor (care of young, building, cleaning)
	+ Major workers: larger, specialized for specific tasks (soldiers have large heads and powerful mandibles)

**Queen Ant Anatomy**

* Larger than worker ants
* Gaster contains reproductive system
* Wings are torn off after mating

**Male Ants**

* Die shortly after mating
* Have wings and muscular mesosoma for flight
* Smaller heads, bigger eyes, and straighter antennae compared to queens

**Ant Life Cycle**

* Individual life cycle: birth, growth, reproduction, death
* Colony life cycle: individual ants cooperate to build nests, find food, and raise offspring (colonies range from a few individuals to millions)

**Ant Anatomy Misconceptions**

* Mesosoma includes thorax and front of abdomen (fused together)
* Abdomen divided into petiole, post-petiole, and gaster

**Key Features**

* Some ants can inject venom through their stings, causing pain and swelling
* Ants have different sizes of workers for various tasks
