**Nest Flooding Response in Cataulacus muticus**

• Researchers studied the behavioral response of Cataulacus muticus ants to nest flooding in a Malaysian rainforest and laboratory settings, observing their unique nesting ecology within hollow internodes of giant bamboo.
• C. muticus nests are prone to flooding due to heavy rains, prompting the ants' two-stage response:
  • Blocking entrances with their heads during heavy rain
  • Drinking water, leaving the nest, and excreting droplets on the stem surface
• This behavior is an adaptive survival mechanism specific to C. muticus's nesting ecology.
• Laboratory experiments with other Cataulacus species (C. catuvolcus and C. horridus) did not exhibit similar water-bailing behaviors.
