**Supercolonies of Ants: Ecological Patterns, Behavioural Processes and Implications for Social Evolution**

* **Key Features of Supercolonies**
	+ Colonies spread by budding, leading to extensive polydomy (multiple queens per colony) and inter-nest movement of individuals
	+ Local mating and recruitment of queens lead to extensive polygyny (multiple males mating with multiple females)
* **Characteristics of Supercolonies**
	+ Span thousands of nests and consist of several million individuals
	+ Have evolved independently in several ant groups (e.g. Hymenoptera: Formicidae)
* **Challenges in Understanding Supercolonies**
	+ Low relatedness among cooperating individuals, making it unclear how cooperation is maintained
	+ Lack of clear functional organization above local units, making it difficult to understand how supercolony-level processes function
* **Review Considerations**
	+ Cooperation and competition are considered at multiple levels, from gene to population level (gene: 25% relatedness, population: polygyny and polydomy)
