**Navigational Cues in Cataglyphis fortis Ants**

• Researchers have found that Cataglyphis fortis ants are able to navigate long distances (over 100 meters) without pheromone trails, using a combination of path integration and visual landmarks for navigation.
• Geocentric navigational cues, including visual landmarks, help the ants pinpoint their nest entrance with precision.

**Olfactory Landmarks**

• Ants have been found to use olfactory landmark information in the form of different odour blends associated with various ground structures.
• When trained to a single odour, ants are able to recognize it within a mixture of four odours, indicating a high level of olfactory sensitivity.

**Visual and Olfactory Integration**

• The use of visual landmarks allows the ants to break down uniform salt-pans into more distinct locations.
• By associating a single odour with their nest entrance, ants can focus their search on that position, suggesting an integration of visual and olfactory information for navigation.
