**Hell Ants: Ancient and Widespread**

* **Discovery**
	+ Found as amber inclusions in France, Myanmar, Canada, and a rock impression in Brazil's Crato Konservat-Lagerstätte
	+ Represents the oldest known Haidomyrmecinae and earliest undisputed ant in the fossil record (Aptian age)
* **Relationships and Distribution**
	+ Closely related to hell ants found in Burmese amber, as shown by micro-computed tomography analysis
	+ Widely distributed across Cretaceous landmasses with repeated interchanges between them
* **Ecosystems and Environments**
	+ Thrived in various landscapes, including:
		- Gymnosperm-dominated ecosystems
		- Mixed ecosystems
