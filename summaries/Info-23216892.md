**Reproductive Strategies in Cataglyphis Ants**

• The reproductive system of Cataglyphis hispanica, C. velox, and C. mauritanica involves hybridogenesis, where workers are produced through sexual reproduction between individuals from different genetic origins.
• In C. hispanica, all workers are sexually produced hybridogens, while queens produce males and females through parthenogenesis, perpetuating only maternal genes across generations.

**Species-Specific Reproductive Strategies**

• Queens of C. velox and C. mauritanica mate with males from a different genetic lineage to produce hybrid workers.
• In contrast, males and female reproductive castes are produced through parthenogenesis in these species.
• Colonies of C. velox and C. mauritanica are typically headed by multiple queens who share the same multilocus genotype.

**Genetic Consequences**

• The social structure of these ants has direct consequences on population genetic variability.
• The reproductive strategies employed by these species also influence caste determination, affecting the development and division of labor within colonies.
