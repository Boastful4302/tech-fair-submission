**Ant Orientation Mechanisms**

* Cataglyphis bicolor ants have two primary mechanisms for orientation: sun orientation and visual pattern recognition.
* **Hunters vs. Diggers**
	+ Hunters prefer terrestrial cues as long as possible before switching to sun orientation.
	+ Diggers rely more on sun orientation due to inferior visual knowledge of their locality compared to hunters.
* **Size Variation among Hunter Ants**
	+ Hunters are typically larger in size than diggers, with significant variation among digger sizes.
	+ The largest and smallest individual hunter ants exhibit different orientation responses:
		- To black/white areas
		- To stripe patterns
