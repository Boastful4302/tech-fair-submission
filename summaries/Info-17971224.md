**Nutritional Contribution of Blochmannia floridanus**

• The genome sequence of Blochmannia floridanus reveals that it produces essential amino acids, which helps upgrade the host's nutrients.
• 90-day feeding experiments showed that worker groups fed a diet lacking essential amino acids had lower brood production, but this was improved when provided with these nutrients.

**Endosymbiont Dynamics and Urease Function**

• Treatment with antibiotics reduced endosymbionts in C. floridanus hosts, as measured by qRT-PCR and FISH.
• Urease function of Blochmannia was confirmed through feeding experiments using 15N-labelled urea, resulting in significant labelling of non-essential and essential amino acids.

**Nitrogen Recycling Role**

• The study suggests that Blochmannia may play a role in nitrogen recycling via its functional urease.
• Blochmannia nutritionally upgrades the diet of C. floridanus hosts by providing essential amino acids, potentially contributing to host fitness.
