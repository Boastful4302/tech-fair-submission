**Division of Labor in Social Insects**

• The division of labor plays a crucial role in the ecological success of social insects.

**Factors Influencing Division of Labor**

• Morphological, genotypic, and age variations among workers
• Social interactions between workers
• Individual experience and learning influence task decision-making
• Research demonstrates that ant workers adapt their behavior based on previous experiences:
	+ Workers who consistently find prey continue to explore for food.
	+ Workers who consistently fail to find prey specialize in brood care.

**Adaptation of Behavior**

• Ant workers learn from their experiences and adjust their tasks accordingly
• This learning process generates a lasting division of labor, potentially contributing to colony efficiency.
