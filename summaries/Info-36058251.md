**Ant Colour Vision**

• Ants are ecologically one of the most important groups of insects with impressive capabilities for visual learning and orientation.
• Studies show ants can learn to discriminate between different colours, regardless of light intensity, and adjust their behavior accordingly.
• Key terms:
	+ Dichromacy: a type of colour vision where an organism has two types of colour receptors
	+ Opsins: the proteins responsible for detecting light in photoreceptors
	+ Spectral sensitivity: the range of wavelengths that can be detected by an organism's visual system
• Colour vision in ants is not yet fully understood, with variable and inconsistent findings across species.
• The review compiles past century's research on molecular, physiological, and behavioural aspects of ant colour vision, which is essential for understanding their complex social behaviour and interactions.
