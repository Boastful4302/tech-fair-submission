**Polydomous vs. Monodomous Colonies**

* Ant colonies with multiple nests (polydomy) may exhibit increased foraging efficiency through dispersed-central place foraging.
* Polydomous colonies discover resources at a higher rate than monodomous (single-nest) colonies, making them more successful in highly dispersed food environments.
* However, polydomy can limit interaction between ants and decrease recruitment success, potentially lowering the relative success of polydomous colonies.
* In contrast, monodomous colonies can rapidly exploit food resources, potentially leading to higher foraging efficiency than polydomous colonies.

**Key Findings**

* Polydomous colonies are more successful in highly dispersed food environments due to increased resource discovery rates.
* Monodomous colonies may have an advantage over polydomous colonies in terms of foraging efficiency and rapid exploitation of food resources.
