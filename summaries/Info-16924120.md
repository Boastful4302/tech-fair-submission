**Trap-Jaw Ant: Odontomachus bauri**

• The mandible strike of the trap-jaw ant Odontomachus bauri can reach speeds of 35-64 m/s in just 0.13 ms.
• This results in forces that exceed 300 times the ant's body weight, due to extreme accelerations.
**Functions and Capabilities**

• The high-speed mandible movements serve two primary functions:
	+ Predation: capturing prey at speeds exceeding those of other documented ballistic predatory appendages
	+ Propulsion: enabling the ant to jump to safety or eject intruders
• The ant's ability to change head orientation and strike surfaces allows it to adapt its trap-jaw mechanism for various tasks.
