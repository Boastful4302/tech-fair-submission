**Altruistic Behavior in Worker Insects**

• Altruistic behavior is observed in some species of worker insects, such as honeybees, where they sacrifice their own reproduction for the benefit of nondescendant kin.
• This behavior can also manifest as suicidal colony defense, a facultative response that occurs when a worker's sting embeds in a predator and breaks off.

**Preemptive Self-Sacrifice in Forelius pusillus**

• In the Brazilian ant species Forelius pusillus, 1-8 workers have been observed sacrificing their lives during nest defense.
• Specifically, these ants sacrifice themselves to close the nest entrance at sunset, demonstrating preemptive self-sacrifice as a novel example of altruistic behavior.
