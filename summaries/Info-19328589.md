**Unicolonial Ants: Extensive Networks and Kin-Selected Behavior**

* Unicolonial ant populations form extensive networks of interconnected nests, spanning hundreds of kilometers.
* These supercolonies exhibit worker altruistic behavior, but this may be detrimental due to its indiscriminate aid to population members rather than relatives.
* Contrary to initial appearances, genetic and behavioral data suggest that unicolonial ants do have colony boundaries defining large kin groups.
* These kin groups are likely family units expressing kin-selected behavior as they grow in size.
* However, kin selection theory predicts that such behaviors become maladaptive and evolutionarily unstable at extreme sizes.
