**Insect Terminology**

• Ants belong to the family Formicidae, a group of colonial hymenopterous insects with complex social organization and various castes.

**Word Definitions**

• **Antenna**: 
	+ A long, slender sensory organ on the head of many insects
• **Claimant**:
	+ One that performs a specified action, often seeking something (e.g. claimant for compensation)
• **Expectorant**:
	+ One promoting coughing up mucus or phlegm
	+ Something acting upon (in a specified manner)
• **Somnambulant**: 
	+ Performing actions while asleep
	+ In a state of sleepwalking
• **Annuitant**:
	+ One receiving regular payments (e.g. annuity)

**Idiomatic Expressions**

• The term "ant" can also refer to an insect that performs a specified action or is connected with something (e.g. "ant in one's pants")
