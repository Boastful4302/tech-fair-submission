**Ant Genome Analysis**

• The genomes of 40 ant species from 10 subfamilies (Formicidae) were studied for size variation.
• Ants have relatively small genomes compared to other insects, with sizes varying three-fold across the family.

**Genome Duplications and Variation**

• Two whole-genome duplications may have occurred in the ancestors of Ectatomma and Apterostigma species.
• The analysis found no significant relationship between genome size and body size.
• Genome sizes range from unknown to 1.3 pg, with a reported error of 146.7 Mb in previous estimates for Solenopsis invicta.

**Species and Analysis Scope**

• This study is the fourth most comprehensive insect family (Formicidae) and order (Hymenoptera) analyzed.
• Key species examined include Linepithema humile, Eciton, Myrmecia, and others from 10 subfamilies.
