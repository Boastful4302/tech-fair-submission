**Animal Evolution and Visual Capabilities**

• Miniaturisation is a common phenomenon in animal evolution that affects various morphological features.
• This process can be linked to potential inferior behavioural capabilities.

**Compound Eye Spatial Resolution**

• Research on five ant species found that compound eye spatial resolution influences obstacle detection and avoidance abilities.
• Higher spatial resolution led to earlier detection of obstacles (10 cm earlier on average).
• Ants with lower spatial resolution resulted in delayed response times when detecting obstacles.
• In extreme cases, ants with the lowest spatial resolution changed direction only when close to the obstacle, indicating impaired visual navigational competence.
