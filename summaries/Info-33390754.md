**Camelosphecia Genus**

• Fossils from mid-Cretaceous Burmite reveal a unique combination of plesiomorphies and apomorphies in "beast ants" (Camelosphecia gen. nov.).

**Taxonomic Classification**

• Phylogenetic analysis positions Camelosphecia as the extinct sister group of the Formicidae family.
• Alate males and females are known, but workers have yet to be recovered.

**Classification and Nomenclature**

• A revised classification includes:
	+ Zigrasimeciina stat. nov.
	+ Myanmyrma maraudera comb. nov.
• Females and males of Camelosphecia exhibit distinct sexual biologies, represented by C. fossor sp. nov. and C. venator sp. nov.

**Key Findings**

• A comprehensive key to major groupings of Mesozoic Formicoidea is provided.
• A synoptic classification of the order is included.

**Sexual Biology and Development**

• Camelosphecia exhibits distinct features between female and male specimens.
• Alate males and females are known, but workers have yet to be recovered.
