**Colony-Specific Signature Mixture and Recognition**

• Ants use colony-specific signature mixtures (labels) present on their cuticle to identify individuals and groups.
• The process of recognition occurs when an ant compares its perceived label to its internal template, known as the colony odor.
• A mismatch between the label and template leads to rejection of the encountered individual.

**Template Acquisition and Learning**

• Template acquisition in ants is still not fully understood, with contradictory evidence about information processing.
• Research suggests that learning may play a role in template formation and nestmate recognition.
