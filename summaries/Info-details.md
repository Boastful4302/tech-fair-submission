**Part I: Nutrition and Circulation**

• Alimentary canal: site of digestion and absorption
• Nutrition: energy obtained through nutrient uptake
• Circulatory system: transports nutrients to cells via blood circulation
• Fat body: stores lipids for energy reserve

**Part II: Locomotion and Movement**

• Thorax: supports legs and wings for mobility
• Legs: used for walking, running, and standing
• Wings: enable flight in flying species
• Muscles: responsible for contraction and relaxation for movement

**Part III: Reproduction and Development**

• Abdomen: stores digestive organs and reproductive structures
• Male reproductive system:
	+ Testes: produce sperm cells
	+ Seminal vesicles: store semen for fertilization
• Female reproductive system:
	+ Ovaries: produce egg cells
	+ Oviducts: transport eggs to uterus
	+ Uterus: supports embryonic development and fetal growth
• Egg formation and embryology
• Postembryonic development: growth and maturation after hatching or birth

**Part IV: Integument, Gas Exchange, and Homeostasis**

• Integument: provides protection and support for the body
• Gaseous exchange:
	+ Oxygen intake through respiration
	+ Carbon dioxide release through exhalation
• Excretion:
	+ Urination: removal of waste products from blood
	+ Defecation: elimination of solid waste
• Temperature regulation: maintaining optimal body temperature

**Part V: Communication and Sensory Perception**

• Nervous system: transmits signals between neurons for communication
• Endocrine system: hormonal signaling for various functions, including growth and development
• Vision:
	+ Detection of light through photoreceptors
	+ Interpretation of color and shape
• Mechanoreception: sensing mechanical stimuli like sound and vibration
• Chemoreception: detecting chemical cues from environment or other individuals
