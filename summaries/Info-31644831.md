**Genetic Reproductive Strategies in Social Insects**

* Genetic studies on social insects have revealed diverse reproductive strategies, including:
	+ Male clonality
	+ Female clonality
	+ Social hybridogenesis (coexistence of two genetic lineages within a population)
	+ Conditional sex use and parthenogenesis

**Cataglyphis Desert Ants as Models**

* Cataglyphis desert ants are useful models due to their unique reproductive systems.
* In several Cataglyphis species, queens conditionally use sex and parthenogenesis to produce:
	+ Sterile workers via parthenogenesis
	+ Reproductive queens via sexual reproduction

**Social Hybridogenesis**

* Social hybridogenesis involves:
	+ Mating between partners of different lineages producing interlineage hybrids (workers)
	+ Asexual production of pure lineage individuals (queens and males) via parthenogenesis.

**Phylogenetic Analyses**

* 11 species were studied, revealing:
	+ One new case of conditional sex use without dependent-lineage reproduction
	+ Five new instances of social hybridogenesis
* Phylogenetic analyses suggest that both conditional sex use and social hybridogenesis have evolved independently multiple times in the genus Cataglyphis.
