**Phylogeny and Eusociality in Aculeate Hymenoptera**

* Eusocial behavior is most notable in aculeate Hymenoptera, which includes ants, bees, and stinging wasps.
* Phylogenetic relationships among major lineages of aculeate Hymenoptera have been contentious.

**New Findings and Implications**

* A new study generates genomic data for a representative series of taxa, resulting in a single well-resolved tree that is robust across multiple methods.
* Key findings:
	+ Apoidea (spheciform wasps and bees) and ants are sister groups, contradicting earlier views.
	+ Vespid wasps are sister to all other aculeates except chrysidoids.
	+ All eusocial species of Hymenoptera are contained within two major groups characterized by nest construction and larval provision transport.

**Evolutionary Implications**

* These findings suggest that transport of larval provisions and nest construction were likely prerequisites for the evolution of eusociality.
