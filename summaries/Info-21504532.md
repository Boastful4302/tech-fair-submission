**Metapleural Gland Functions and Evolution**

• The metapleural gland (MG) is a unique, complex structure found in ants, suggesting its importance in their evolution and ecological success.
• Key functions:
	+ Sanitation
	+ Chemical defense
	+ Recognition odor (possible)
	+ Territorial marking (possible)
• MG's design is unusual among insects, with secretions stored in a rigid invagination and released passively or through grooming.
• Repeated loss of the MG has occurred in ants, particularly in Formicinae and Myrmicinae subfamilies.
• Males are more likely to lack the MG than workers.
• Limited research on MG chemistry restricts understanding of its origins.
• A comprehensive approach is needed to understand the evolution of the MG in ants.
