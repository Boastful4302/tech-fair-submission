**Ants: A Complex Relationship with their Environment**

• Ants are often viewed as predators/scavengers, but isotopic studies reveal many dominant species are functional herbivores.

**Symbiotic Relationships with Bacteria**

• Symbiotic bacteria may supplement nutritional needs of nitrogen-poor diets in ants and other insects.
• Cloning and 16S sequencing identified bacterial flora in herbivorous ants from different trophic levels.
• Key findings:
	+ Predatory and herbivorous ants have distinct bacterial communities.
	+ Similar microbial communities are found among distantly related herbivorous ants.
	+ Some predatory army ant species share similar communities.

**Core Microbiota in Herbivorous Ants**

• Five major bacterial taxa were identified as the core microbiota in one herbivorous ant tribe (Cephalotini).
• Bacterial relatives may play roles in fixing, recycling, or upgrading nitrogen.
