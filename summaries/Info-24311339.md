**Imported Fire Ants' Venom Dispersal Mechanisms**

• Imported fire ants (Solenopsis invicta) use a unique mechanism called "gaster flagging" to disperse their venom through the air.
• This behavior is distinct from any other ant species and involves raising and vibrating the ant's gaster.
  
**Venom Release Quantities**

• Foraging workers release up to 500 ng of air-dispersed venom when encountering heterospecific ants during foraging.
• Brood tenders dispense smaller quantities (approximately 1 ng) of the venom onto the brood surface, which may serve as an antibiotic.

**Behavioral Control and Context-Specificity**

• Workers can control the quantity of venom released and exhibit context-specific behavior rather than temporal caste-specific behavior.
