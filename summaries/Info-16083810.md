**Introduction**

• The study on ant venom immunotherapy (VIT) was conducted by Simon G A Brown, Robert J Heddle, Michael D Wiese, and Konrad E Blackman in 2005.

**Methodology**

• A double-blind method was used to assess the effectiveness of VIT.
• Immunologic desensitization using ant venoms was employed.

**Efficacy**

• Ant venom immunotherapy has been linked to reduced symptoms of insect bites and stings.
• The study showed potential for treatment using whole body extracts in addition to VIT.
