**Desert Ant Navigation**

* Studies have been conducted on desert ant (Cataglyphis) navigation to understand its behavioral performances, neural mechanisms, and ecological functions.
* Key navigational tools used by Cataglyphis include:
	+ Skylight (polarization) compass
	+ Path integrator (primarily relied upon as an egocentric guideline)
	+ View-dependent place recognition and landmark route following
	+ Flexible interlinking of navigation modes
* The path integration approach uses procedural knowledge, with context-dependent retrieval, rather than geocentred representations of space.
