**Path Integration in Desert Ants**

• Desert ants (Cataglyphis) use path integration to navigate their desert habitat.
• Path integration involves integrating directions steered and distances traveled.
• **Step Integrator Hypothesis**: A hypothesis that proposes ants measure distance by counting steps.

**Experimental Evidence for Step Integrator Hypothesis**

• Experimental manipulation of leg lengths affected stride length:
	+ Elongated legs (stilts): larger strides, resulting in overestimated travel distance
	+ Shortened legs (stumps): smaller strides, leading to underestimated travel distance
