**Directed Aerial Descent of Cephalotes atratus Ants**

* Cephalotes atratus ants use directed aerial descent to return home with >80% success during a fall.
* Ants descend abdomen-first through steep glide trajectories at relatively high velocities, using visual cues to locate tree trunks before hitting the forest floor.

**Factors Affecting Successful Directed Descent**

* Smaller workers and species regain contact with their associated tree trunk over shorter vertical distances than larger ones.
* The ability to perform directed descent is not unique to Cephalotes atratus ants; it occurs in most Cephalotini and Pseudomyrmecinae, but not in ponerimorphs or Dolichoderinae ants.
