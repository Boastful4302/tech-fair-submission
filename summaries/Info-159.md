**Classification and Distribution**

• Approximately 435 species in North America, with most found in the Southwest.
• Worldwide, there are about 230 genera (or subgenera) and 8,000 species of velvet ants.

**Physical Characteristics and Behavior**

• Females are wingless and hairy, resembling large ants.
• Males have wings and are less hairy.
• Adults take nectar as a food source.
• Males were observed taking nectar.
• Males are larger than females.

**Behavioral Ecology and Defense Mechanisms**

• Velvet ants are parasitoids of other solitary hymenoptera (bees and wasps), sometimes attacking beetles or flies.
• Handling velvet ants can result in an excruciating sting.
