Clowning Framework, the

Word Type: 
Noun
Subject Group: 
Drama-Clowning

Clowning Framework the noun [Clowning] A clowning framework is the general story line a sketch follows. Frameworks give clowns a clowns a beginning, an idea of what takes place in the middle, and an ending for the act. The clowns can then fill in the specific bits and gags they will use to fill out the rest of the sketch or act
